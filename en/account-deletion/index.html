Deleting the account

Last updated: 15 November 2026

App: slobodan · Developer: Slobodan Trifunović

Deleting is permanentAfter deleting, the data cannot be restored, not even by support.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the slobodan app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings › Business profile.
  3. At the bottom of the screen, tap “Delete account”.
  4. Read what gets deleted and tap “Delete account”.
  5. Confirm with “Delete for good”.

What gets deleted

Sign-in account
Your email and the details of the account you sign in with
Business
Profile, services, working hours, logo
Clients
Directory, bookings, visits, packages and payments
Notes and measurements
Everything you wrote about your clients
Link with the client app
Clients will no longer see your business

If you sign in again with the same account, you start over with an empty business.

How long

  • While the account is active.
  • When you delete your account, the data is deleted right away.
  • When you delete a client, their notes and measurements are deleted too.

Without the app

Send an email to stit.apps@gmail.com from the address of the account you want to delete, with the subject “Account deletion”. We will delete the account with all the data listed here and let you know when it is done.
