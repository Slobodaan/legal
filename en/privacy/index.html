Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

The slobodan app is provided by Slobodan Trifunović, Nehruova 170, Belgrade, Serbia, tel. 0644994276, email: stit.apps@gmail.com. For any questions about your data, write to stit.apps@gmail.com.

2. Two roles

For your account and your business, we decide how the data is processed and we are responsible for it.

For the data about your clients (directory, bookings, visits, payments, packages, notes, measurements), you decide. We store and process it only so that the app works for you, as set out in the Terms of Service.

3. What data we keep

  • Your account: the email and name of the account you sign in with (Google, Apple or email).
  • Your business: name, type of business, address and location, contact details and links, logo, working hours, days off, services, prices and packages.
  • Your clients: name, phone, note, bookings, visits and absences, packages, payments, notes and measurements.
  • Client app: the client's email and their link to your business.
  • Technical data: phone model, app version, usage events without names or phone numbers, crash and performance reports.

4. Why

  • So that the app works: sign-in, booking, packages, payments, notes.
  • To find and fix errors and make the app faster.

We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

5. Notes and measurements

Notes and measurements may contain health data. Therefore:
• only the business owner sees them;
• they are not sent to analytics, crash reports or notifications;
• the client does not see them in their app;
• they are deleted together with the client and with the account.

Data is transferred over an encrypted connection and stored on an encrypted disk. Notes are not encrypted with a separate key held only by the owner, so whoever maintains the database can technically access them; we do so only when it is necessary for maintenance or at your request.

If you enter health data, you are required to have a basis for it, for example the client's consent.

6. Who else processes data

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, logo storage.
  • Google (Firebase): crash reports, performance, app settings.
  • Google: sign-in with a Google account.
  • Google (Gmail): sending emails for sign-up and password reset.
  • Apple: sign-in with an Apple account (iPhone).

7. Where data is stored

The database is in the European Union, in Ireland. Google and Apple may also process data outside Serbia and the EU, with the protection required by law.

8. How long

  • While the account is active.
  • When you delete your account, the data is deleted right away.
  • When you delete a client, their notes and measurements are deleted too.

9. Your rights

You have the right to know what data we keep, to correct it, delete it, download it, restrict its processing and object. Write to stit.apps@gmail.com. You can also contact the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (www.poverenik.rs).

A client of a business contacts the business about their data; we help the owner respond.

10. Deleting the account

In the app: Settings › Business profile › Delete account. Without the app: https://slobodaan.github.io/legal/en/account-deletion/ or an email to stit.apps@gmail.com.

11. Minors

Only an adult can open an owner account. If you enter a minor as a client, you are responsible for the parent's consent.

12. Changes

We announce important changes in the app before they take effect.
